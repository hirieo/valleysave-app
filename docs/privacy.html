ValleySave · Last updated June 2026

Privacy and data usage

ValleySave is an app for syncing your Stardew Valley saves with your own Google Drive account.

What data it uses

ValleySave only accesses the Stardew Valley save files necessary to make copies, sync them, or restore them.

Google Drive access

Your saves are uploaded to your own Google Drive account, inside a ValleySave/ folder. ValleySave doesn't use its own servers to store your games — there is no ValleySave server at all.

ValleySave requests Google's full Drive scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive). This is required so that sharing farms with other players works — reading a farm someone else shared with you needs broader access than the narrower drive.file scope allows.

Despite that permission, the app's code only ever reads or writes its own ValleySave/ folder and folders explicitly shared with you. It never looks at anything else in your Drive, on its own or otherwise.

Permissions on Android

ValleySave may need storage permissions or tools like Shizuku to access save files.

Ads

ValleySave may show ads through Google AdMob.

Responsibility

Your save files are your responsibility.

Delete data

You can delete your synchronized copies by manually deleting the ValleySave folder from Google Drive.
